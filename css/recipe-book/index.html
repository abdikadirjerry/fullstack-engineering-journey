Easy Recipe: Creamy Garlic Pasta

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup cream
  • 1/2 cup parmesan
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta until tender.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  3. Add garlic and cook briefly.
  4. Add cream and parmesan.
  5. Mix the pasta with the sauce.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.

Cooking Tips

Prepare Ingredients First

Measure and prepare everything before starting to cook.

Taste as You Cook

Taste your food during cooking and adjust seasoning when necessary.

Keep It Simple

Fresh ingredients and simple techniques can create excellent meals.
